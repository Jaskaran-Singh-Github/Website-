Conditions We Treat

Understanding your pain is the first step towards recovery.

Sciatica & Back Pain

Shooting pain, numbness, or tingling originating from the lower back and traveling down the leg. We use targeted therapies to decompress the nerve and strengthen the core to prevent recurrence.

Arthritis & Joint Pain

Inflammation of joints causing pain and stiffness. While arthritis cannot be cured, our physiotherapy programs significantly reduce pain, preserve joint function, and improve mobility.

Sports Injuries

Sprains, strains, tennis elbow, and runner's knee. We provide aggressive yet safe rehabilitation to get athletes back to their sport stronger than before.

Neck Pain & Poor Posture

Often caused by prolonged sitting ("tech neck") leading to stiffness and headaches. We focus on ergonomic correction, manual therapy, and strengthening postural muscles.
